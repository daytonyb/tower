How to Play

  • Aim & Fire: Use mouse to aim, Click to fire.
  • Ammo: Limited shots that recharge over time.
  • Level Up: Kill zombies for XP to get traps.
  • Place Traps: Scroll Wheel or R/E to rotate.
  • Gold: Spend it in the shop for permanent upgrades.

Helpful Tips

  • Place Plague Totems further away from the tower.
  • Combine Barbed Wire with Tar Pits.
  • Combine Soul Siphons for big XP gains.
  • Place Healing Wards on top of each other.
  • Wooden Walls start weak, but have many related upgrades.
  • Press ESC to pause mid-run!

Patch Notes (v0.10.0)

  • Metaprogression Overhaul: The placement phase has been completely removed to make runs start significantly faster!
  • New Upgrades: Blueprint Prestige mechanics have been added (Fossilized Pitch, Rending Barbs, etc.).
  • Dynamic Weather: Defeating bosses now triggers powerful weather events (Acid Rain, Heatwaves, and Thunderstorms) that alter the battlefield!
  • Enemy Diversity: The horde grows stronger with new Ranged Skeletons, speedy Swarmers, and tanky Brutes entering the fray.
  • Bug Fixes: Numerous bug fixes and quality-of-life improvements have been implemented to enhance gameplay.
